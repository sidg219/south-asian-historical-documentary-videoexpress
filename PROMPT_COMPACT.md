# Compact instructions

Produce a faceless narrated historical documentary about ancient or medieval India. Start from a source-backed research brief and claim ledger. For every scene label the status as documented, interpreted or reconstructed. Use period-specific World Locks for place, materials, clothing, architecture, weather and light. Keep a single consistent narrator voice; do not show a talking head. Use present-day establishing shots, maps, objects, architecture, craft processes, hands, silhouettes and carefully labelled visual reconstructions. Never invent quotations or imply that generated images are archival evidence. Deliver narration, shot list, image/video prompts, source notes, uncertainty notes and an export checklist.
