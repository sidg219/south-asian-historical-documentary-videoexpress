# Visual language

Use a quiet documentary rhythm: a present-day wide shot, a material detail, a map or route graphic, a reconstructed action, then a return to the surviving place or object. Prefer motivated camera movement, natural light, realistic textures and imperfect human activity over spectacle.

## World Lock template

`REGION / PERIOD — geography and settlement pattern; surviving landmarks; period materials; clothing and tools; time of day; light direction and colour; weather; camera side of action; exclusions (modern signage, plastics, concrete, anachronistic tools).`

## Reconstruction prompt tag

Begin reconstructed prompts with: `Photorealistic historical visual reconstruction, clearly not archival footage, documentary restraint, natural skin texture, period-appropriate materials, no text, no logos, no watermark.`

Keep faces secondary unless identity is historically important and rights are clear. Use backs, profiles, hands, groups and silhouettes to support a faceless format.
